For athletes

Your game,
measured.

Film a session, get the numbers behind it, and see whether this week moved better than last.

  • Strokes, counted

    Every swing in the clip is found and counted for you.

  • Forehand or backhand

    Recognised from the motion — one- or two-handed, left- or right-handed. You are never asked.

  • Swing range and knee bend

    Measured per stroke against a target, so you know which shot to work on.

  • Readiness, over time

    One score per session and its trend across the last weeks.

  • Replay with your skeleton

    Watch the stroke back beside the body APeX rebuilt from it.
